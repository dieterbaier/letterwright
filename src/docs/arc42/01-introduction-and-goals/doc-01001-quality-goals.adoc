[[quality-goals]]
== Quality Goals

[cols="1,2,4", options="header"]
|===
| ID | Goal | Rationale

| QG-001
| Accessibility and standards conformance
| Preview and saved document are accessible; saved documents are
  PDF/A-conformant (xref:qs-001-pdfa-konformitaet[QS-001], xref:qs-002-barrierefreies-pdf[QS-002],
  xref:qs-003-barrierefreie-vorschau[QS-003]).

| QG-002
| Fidelity
| Preview and saved document look the same; a business letterhead complies with
  DIN 5008 (xref:qs-004-wiedergabetreue-vorschau-und-pdf[QS-004],
  xref:qs-005-din-5008-masshaltigkeit[QS-005]).

| QG-003
| Maintainability by business users
| Non-technical people maintain letterheads and letter templates
  (xref:qs-006-briefpapier-pflege-durch-fachanwender[QS-006]).

| QG-004
| Integrability
| The library can be integrated into existing applications with little effort
  (xref:qs-007-integration-in-host-anwendung[QS-007]).

| QG-005
| Traceability
| Saved letters stay unchanged and can be assigned to their template versions
  (xref:qs-008-unveraenderliche-schreiben[QS-008]).
|===

Testability with 80 % coverage is a binding constraint
