== QS-005: DIN 5008 Dimensional Accuracy of the Business Letterhead

=== Status

Proposed.

The response measure is a reviewable target. Treat uncertain values as
assumptions until confirmed.

=== Scenario

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Element | Description
| Source | Template manager
| Stimulus | Creates a business letterhead in form B (default) or form A.
| Environment | Normal operation.
| Artifact | Letterhead building blocks and generated PDF.
| Response | The letterhead keeps the dimensions of the chosen form, even when the logo and texts are changed.
| Response Measure | Deviation of the address field and fold mark positions in the PDF ≤ 0.5 mm compared with DIN 5008, checked automatically.
|===

=== Rationale

Window envelopes and folding require exact positions.

=== Assumptions

* The private letterhead is not bound to the standard.
