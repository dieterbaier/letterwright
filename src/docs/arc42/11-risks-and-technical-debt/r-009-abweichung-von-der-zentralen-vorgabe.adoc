== R-009: Deviation from the Central DOCX Specification

=== Status

Proposed.

This risk was identified with AI assistance. It names a topic for review and
does not decide risk acceptance.

=== Risk Statement

Because the style specifications from the central DOCX specification are transferred manually into the letterhead, later changes to the specification may go unnoticed, leading to letters that no longer match the current appearance.

=== Assessment

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Value
| Likelihood | Medium.
| Impact | Medium.
| Priority | Medium.
| Timeframe | Medium-term.
| Confidence | Low.
|===

How often the specification changes is unknown (Q-PO-009).

=== Mitigation Options

* Keep the state or identifier of the specification as an attribute on the letterhead.
* Process: the central department reports changes to the template managers.
* Later: import of the style values from DOCX styles, to avoid transfer errors.

=== Decision or Acceptance

The risk remains under analysis until the accountable owner confirms the
assessment and mitigation.
