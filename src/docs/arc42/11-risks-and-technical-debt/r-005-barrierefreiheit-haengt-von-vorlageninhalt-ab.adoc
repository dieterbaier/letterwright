== R-005: Accessibility Depends on the Template Content

=== Status

Proposed.

This risk was identified with AI assistance. It names a topic for review and
does not decide risk acceptance.

=== Risk Statement

Because business users maintain content, logos without alternative text or headings as bold text may arise, leading to letters that are not accessible.

=== Assessment

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Value
| Likelihood | High.
| Impact | Medium.
| Priority | Medium.
| Timeframe | Near-term.
| Confidence | High.
|===

A typical problem of editorial systems.

=== Mitigation Options

* The editor enforces alternative texts for images.
* Check of the template on publishing with axe-core and veraPDF.

=== Decision or Acceptance

The risk remains under analysis until the accountable owner confirms the
assessment and mitigation.
