== R-002: Deviations Between the Preview and PDF Engines

=== Status

Proposed.

This risk was identified with AI assistance. It names a topic for review and
does not decide risk acceptance.

=== Risk Statement

Because the preview and the PDF are produced by two different layout engines, page breaks, hyphenation or spacing may differ, leading to the saved letter not matching the preview.

=== Assessment

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Value
| Likelihood | Medium.
| Impact | High.
| Priority | High.
| Timeframe | Near-term.
| Confidence | Low.
|===

Without a prototype, the extent cannot be estimated; simple letter layouts lower the likelihood.

=== Mitigation Options

* Fidelity test from QS-004 for all reference templates.
* The same embedded fonts, no automatic hyphenation.
* Candidate Chromium for both paths (ADR-004, option 2), only after proof of the same acceptance criteria.

=== Decision or Acceptance

The risk remains under analysis until the accountable owner confirms the
assessment and mitigation.
