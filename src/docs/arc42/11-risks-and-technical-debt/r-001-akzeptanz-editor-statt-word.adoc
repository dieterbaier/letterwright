== R-001: Acceptance of an Editor Instead of Word

=== Status

Proposed.

This risk was identified with AI assistance. It names a topic for review and
does not decide risk acceptance.

=== Risk Statement

Because business users work with DOCX files today, a new, building-block-based editor may be rejected, leading to template maintenance by developers or to a demand for DOCX support.

=== Assessment

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Value
| Likelihood | Low.
| Impact | High.
| Priority | Medium.
| Timeframe | Near-term.
| Confidence | Medium.
|===

The owner has confirmed that an editor instead of Word is fine (Q-PO-005). Residual risk: acceptance by the actual business users has not been tested yet.

=== Mitigation Options

* Prototype with real DOCX templates and business users before accepting ADR-003.
* Plan a DOCX import for text content as an extension.

=== Decision or Acceptance

The risk remains under analysis until the accountable owner confirms the
assessment and mitigation.
